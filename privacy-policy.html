SmartMoney Privacy Policy

Version 1.1, draft for approval. Last updated 16 September 2026.

This policy explains what personal information the SmartMoney app collects, why, where it is stored, who else processes it, how long it is kept, and how to exercise your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). It describes the app as it works today. It is not legal advice.

3. Why we process it

  • To provide the service you signed up for: your tax diagnosis, estimates, tasks, deadline reminders, reports, Business Suite and Sam.
  • Your ID number or passport number is used to confirm your identity, work out age-based rebates and prevent duplicate accounts.
  • To manage your subscription and the practitioner minutes that come with it.
  • To send the emails the service needs: account confirmation, password reset and, when you press send, invoices and reports. Feedback, waiting-list and quote requests are emailed to the SmartMoney team so they can respond to you.
  • To answer support requests and keep the app working, including fixing crashes.

The lawful basis is performing the service you asked for and your consent, which you give when you complete your profile. We do not sell personal information and do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Sam does not use outside AI

Sam answers from a library of approved answers written for SmartMoney. It runs on our own backend and no outside artificial intelligence provider receives your messages. The content of your messages is not written to our logs. Sam refuses messages that contain long numbers such as an ID, tax, card or phone number, so that those numbers are not stored in your conversation.

Voice input is optional. When you tap the microphone, speech recognition is performed by your phone's operating system: on iPhone by Apple and on Android by Google, under their own privacy terms. The app receives only the recognised text, and does not record or store audio.

6. How we protect it

  • All connections between the app and our servers are encrypted in transit.
  • Our hosting provider encrypts stored data at rest.
  • Database access rules mean only your signed-in account can read or change your records.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept in your device's secure storage. You can add a Face ID or fingerprint lock, and the app signs you out after a short time in the background unless that lock is on.
  • Your ID, passport and tax numbers can be hidden on screen from your profile and are never written to our logs.

7. How long we keep it

  • While your account exists: we keep your information so the service works. Some financial records you delete in the app are marked as deleted and hidden rather than erased, so that linked records stay consistent; they are erased when your account is deleted.
  • When you delete your account: your profile, every record listed in section 2, your Sam conversations and your uploaded files are permanently erased, and your sign-in is removed.
  • Backups: our hosting provider keeps daily database backups for 7 days, so deleted information can remain in a backup for up to 7 days before it is overwritten.
  • Crash reports are kept by Firebase for 90 days.
  • Purchase records remain with Apple or Google and with RevenueCat under their own policies and financial record-keeping duties.

8. Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask whether we hold personal information about you and request access to it, ask us to correct or delete it, object to its processing, and withdraw consent.

  • Delete your account: in the app, open your Profile and choose Delete account. This erases your information as described in section 7.
  • Correct your details: edit them in your profile.
  • Everything else, including a copy of your information: contact the Information Officer at support@getsmartmoney.co.za. We will respond within a reasonable time and in any event within the periods POPIA sets.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za.

9. Information about other people

If you add customers in the Business Suite, you are responsible for having a lawful reason to hold and use their details. We process that information on your behalf only to provide the Business Suite to you, and it is erased with your account.

10. Age

SmartMoney is intended for people aged 18 and over.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, the app will show the change before it takes effect. The date at the top is the date of the current version.
